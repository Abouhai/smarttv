Installation Guide

How to Install Smart IPTV on Any Device

Get started in minutes with the SmartOne IPTV app. Follow the step-by-step guide for your device below.

📺 Samsung Smart TV

  1. Open Smart Hub on your Samsung TV
  2. Go to Apps and search for SmartOne IPTV
  3. Install the app and open it
  4. Enter the M3U URL or Xtream credentials we send you via WhatsApp
  5. Enjoy your channels!
Full guide →

📺 LG Smart TV

  1. Open LG Content Store
  2. Search for SmartOne IPTV or use the browser
  3. Visit smartone-iptv.com for the webOS app
  4. Enter your credentials from us
  5. Start streaming immediately
Full guide →

📱 Android / Android TV

  1. Open Google Play Store
  2. Search for SmartOne IPTV and install
  3. Or visit smartone-iptv.com for the APK
  4. Enter your M3U URL or Xtream login
  5. Browse 32,000+ channels
Full guide →

🍎 iPhone / iPad (iOS)

  1. Open the App Store
  2. Search SmartOne IPTV and download
  3. Launch the app
  4. Add your playlist using the M3U URL we provide
  5. All channels load automatically
Full guide →

🔥 Amazon Firestick

  1. Go to Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options
  2. Enable Apps from Unknown Sources
  3. Install Downloader app from Amazon Store
  4. Enter the SmartOne APK link from smartone-iptv.com
  5. Install and enter your credentials
Full guide →

💻 PC / Mac / Browser

  1. Visit smartone-iptv.com
  2. Download the Windows or Mac player
  3. Or use VLC Player — open M3U URL from network
  4. Paste your playlist URL from us
  5. All channels stream in your browser or app
Full guide →
