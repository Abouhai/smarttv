How to Install Smart IPTV on Any Device

Complete step-by-step setup guide for the SmartOne IPTV app on every major device. Get started in under 5 minutes.

Home› How to Install
📦 What you'll need: After subscribing or requesting your free trial via WhatsApp, we'll send you your M3U URL or Xtream Codes (username, password, server URL). Have this ready before starting the install steps below.

📺 Samsung Smart TV

Method 1 — SmartOne App (Recommended)

  1. Open Smart Hub

    Press the Home button on your Samsung remote and navigate to Smart Hub.

  2. Go to Apps

    Select Apps from the menu and click on the Search icon in the top-right corner.

  3. Search SmartOne IPTV

    Type SmartOne in the search bar and press Enter. Select the SmartOne IPTV app from the results.

  4. Install the App

    Click Install and wait for the download to complete. Then click Open.

  5. Enter Your Credentials

    Choose M3U URL or Xtream Codes and enter the details we sent you via WhatsApp.

  6. Load Your Channels

    The app will load your 32,000+ channels and EPG automatically. Enjoy streaming!

Method 2 — Smart IPTV App (Alternative)

  1. Open Samsung App Store

    Go to Smart Hub → Apps and search for Smart IPTV.

  2. Install and get your MAC address

    Install the app, open it, and note the MAC address shown on screen.

  3. Visit siptv.eu

    On a PC or phone, go to siptv.eu/mylist/ and enter your MAC address + M3U URL.

  4. Restart the app

    Return to your TV and restart the Smart IPTV app. Your channels will load automatically.

📺 LG Smart TV

SmartOne IPTV on LG webOS

  1. Open LG Content Store

    Press the Home button on your LG remote and navigate to LG Content Store.

  2. Search for SmartOne

    Use the search function to find SmartOne IPTV. If not available, use the browser method below.

  3. Alternative: Use LG Browser

    Open the LG browser and visit smartone-iptv.com to find the webOS compatible version.

  4. Install and Open

    Install the app and open it from your home screen.

  5. Add Your Playlist

    Enter your M3U URL or Xtream Codes from your WhatsApp message from us.

  6. Start Watching

    Your channels and EPG will load. Press the channel list to start browsing 32,000+ channels.

📱 Android Phone, Tablet & Android TV

SmartOne IPTV on Android

  1. Open Google Play Store

    On your Android device or Android TV, open the Google Play Store.

  2. Search SmartOne IPTV

    Search for SmartOne IPTV and tap Install. Alternatively, visit smartone-iptv.com to download the APK directly.

  3. Open the App

    Launch SmartOne IPTV from your app drawer or home screen.

  4. Add Your Playlist

    Tap Add Playlist or Xtream Codes and enter the credentials we sent via WhatsApp.

  5. Browse Your Channels

    All 32,000+ channels will load. Use the EPG to see what's on and when.

🍎 iPhone & iPad (iOS)

SmartOne IPTV on iPhone / iPad

  1. Open App Store

    Open the App Store on your iPhone or iPad.

  2. Search SmartOne IPTV

    Search for SmartOne IPTV and tap the download button. It's free to install.

  3. Launch the App

    Open SmartOne IPTV from your home screen.

  4. Add Your M3U Playlist

    Tap the + button or Add Playlist, then enter your M3U URL or Xtream login from our WhatsApp message.

  5. Load and Watch

    Your channels load automatically with full EPG support. Swipe through categories to find your favourite content.

🔥 Amazon Firestick & Fire TV

SmartOne IPTV on Firestick

  1. Enable Unknown Sources

    Go to Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options and turn on Apps from Unknown Sources and ADB Debugging.

  2. Install Downloader App

    Search the Amazon Appstore for Downloader (orange icon) and install it. This lets you sideload APKs.

  3. Get the SmartOne APK URL

    Visit smartone-iptv.com on your phone/PC to get the direct APK download link for Firestick.

  4. Download via Downloader

    Open Downloader on your Firestick, enter the APK URL, and download. Click Install when prompted.

  5. Open SmartOne IPTV

    Find the app in Your Apps & Channels. Open it and enter your credentials from our WhatsApp message.

  6. Enjoy Streaming

    All your channels are ready. Use the remote to navigate the EPG and channel list.

📡 MAG Box

Smart IPTV on MAG Box (Stalker Portal)

  1. Power On Your MAG Box

    Connect your MAG box to your TV via HDMI and power it on.

  2. Go to Settings

    Navigate to Settings → System Settings → Servers.

  3. Enter Portal URL

    Under Portals, enter the Portal URL we provide you via WhatsApp after subscribing.

  4. Save and Restart

    Save your settings and restart the MAG box. It will load the portal automatically.

  5. Browse Channels

    Once loaded, you can browse all channels, VOD and EPG directly on your MAG box.

💻 PC, Mac & Web Browser

VLC Media Player

  1. Download VLC

    Download and install VLC Media Player (free, available for Windows & Mac).

  2. Open Network Stream

    Go to Media → Open Network Stream (Ctrl+N on Windows, Cmd+N on Mac).

  3. Paste M3U URL

    Paste the M3U URL we sent you via WhatsApp and click Play.

  4. Browse Channels

    Use the playlist panel to browse and select channels.

SmartOne Desktop App

  1. Visit SmartOne Website

    Go to smartone-iptv.com and download the Windows or Mac desktop player.

  2. Install the App

    Run the installer and follow the on-screen steps.

  3. Enter Credentials

    Add your M3U URL or Xtream Codes provided by us via WhatsApp.

  4. Start Streaming

    All 32,000+ channels load with full EPG support on your desktop.
